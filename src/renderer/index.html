      Server backups

      BACKUPS

      Your world is backed up automatically while the server runs, and once more when you stop it. A backup holds your world(s), server.jar and player lists. Restoring also restores its server.jar when included; older backups leave your installed jar alone. Mods, plugins and their settings are not included.
